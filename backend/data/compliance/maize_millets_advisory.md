# Maize & Millets - ICAR Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR), Ludhiana; ICAR-Indian Institute of Millets Research (IIMR), Hyderabad
**Crops:** Maize, Pearl Millet (Bajra), Finger Millet (Ragi), Sorghum (Jowar), Foxtail Millet
**Regions:** All India

---

## Maize (Zea mays)

### Varieties
- **Kharif composites:** Navjot, Prabhat, Kisan, Shweta (sweet corn)
- **Kharif hybrids:** HQPM-1, HQPM-5, DHM 117, DHM 121, Vivek QPM-9 (high protein)
- **Rabi (winter maize):** DHM 117, P 3522, NK 6240 — Bihar, West Bengal, AP
- **Baby corn:** HM-4, VL Baby Corn 1

### Sowing
- Kharif: June-July; Rabi: October-November; Spring: January-February
- Seed rate: 20 kg/hectare (hybrids); 15 kg/hectare (sweet corn)
- Spacing: 60x20 cm (kharif); 60x25 cm (rabi)
- Seed treatment: Thiram 75% WP at 3g/kg seed

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- Kharif: N:P:K = 120:60:40 kg
- Rabi/Spring: N:P:K = 150:60:40 kg (higher N for longer growing season)
- Apply 1/3 N + full P + full K basal; 1/3 N at knee-high; 1/3 N at tasseling
- ZnSO4: 25 kg/hectare as basal

### Major Diseases
- **Turcicum Leaf Blight (Exserohilum turcicum):** Long elliptical grey-green lesions. Spray Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre
- **Maydis Leaf Blight:** Smaller, rectangular buff-coloured lesions. Spray Zineb at 2.5g/litre
- **Post-Flowering Stalk Rot (Macrophomina/Fusarium):** Sudden drying after flowering; stalk becomes hollow. No cure — avoid water stress; balanced fertilization
- **Downy Mildew:** Chlorotic streaks on leaves; white downy growth. Seed treatment with Metalaxyl 35% WS at 6g/kg

### Major Pests
- **Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda):** See separate detailed advisory (fall_armyworm_maize.md)
- **Stem Borer (Chilo partellus):** Dead heart; shot holes on leaves. Apply Carbofuran 3G at 8-10 granules/whorl; spray Chlorantraniliprole at 0.3ml/litre
- **Cob Borer (Helicoverpa armigera):** Silk channel feeding; cob damage. Spray Emamectin benzoate at 0.4g/litre at silk emergence

## Pearl Millet / Bajra (Pennisetum glaucum)

### Varieties
- **Hybrids:** HHB 67 Improved, GHB 905, RHB 177, ICTP 8203
- **Composites:** WC-C75, ICMV 221, Raj 171 — for seed saving by farmers
- **Dual purpose (grain + fodder):** Proagro 9450, HHB 226

### Sowing
- Kharif: With onset of monsoon (June-July)
- Summer: February-March (Gujarat, Rajasthan with irrigation)
- Seed rate: 4-5 kg/hectare (hybrids); 3.5-4 kg/hectare (composites)
- Spacing: 45x15 cm

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- Rainfed: N:P = 40:20 kg
- Irrigated: N:P:K = 80:40:40 kg
- FYM: 5 tonnes/hectare
- Bajra is drought-tolerant and grows on poor soils — ideal for arid Rajasthan/Gujarat

### Major Diseases
- **Downy Mildew (Sclerospora graminicola):** Green ear/crazy top; leafy inflorescence instead of grain. Seed treatment with Metalaxyl 35% WS at 6g/kg. Use resistant hybrids. MOST IMPORTANT DISEASE
- **Ergot (Claviceps fusiformis):** Honeydew dripping from florets; replaced by dark sclerotia. Remove infected earheads; deep ploughing
- **Smut (Moesziomyces penicillariae):** Green sori on individual grains. Use resistant varieties; seed treatment with Carboxin

### Major Pests
- **Shoot Fly (Atherigona approximata):** Dead heart in seedlings. Early sowing reduces damage; seed treatment with Imidacloprid
- **Stem Borer:** Same as maize — Carbofuran in whorl

## Finger Millet / Ragi (Eleusine coracana)

### Varieties
- **Karnataka:** GPU 28, GPU 67, MR 6, ML 365
- **Tamil Nadu:** CO 15, Paiyur 2
- **Uttarakhand/hills:** VL 149, VL 324, VL 376, PRM 1

### Sowing
- Kharif: June-July (transplanting after 25-30 day nursery)
- Seed rate: 5 kg/hectare (transplanting); 10 kg/hectare (direct sowing)
- Spacing: 30x10 cm (transplanting)

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- N:P:K = 50:40:25 kg (irrigated); 25:20:12 (rainfed)
- Ragi responds well to FYM — apply 10 tonnes/hectare
- Apply Azospirillum + PSB as seed treatment

### Major Diseases
- **Blast (Magnaporthe grisea):** Affects leaves, neck, and fingers. Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP at 0.6g/litre at boot leaf and flowering
- **Smut:** Dark masses on individual grains. Seed treatment with Carbendazim

### Nutritional Value (per 100g grain)
- Calcium: 344 mg (HIGHEST among all cereals — excellent for bone health)
- Iron: 3.9 mg; Protein: 7.3g; Fibre: 11.5g
- **Recommended for:** Diabetics (low glycemic index), pregnant women (calcium), anemia (iron)

## Sorghum / Jowar (Sorghum bicolor)

### Varieties
- **Kharif grain:** CSH 16, SPH 1655, CSH 25, Parbhani Moti (M 35-1 — rabi)
- **Rabi grain:** M 35-1, Phule Revati, Phule Vasudha
- **Fodder:** CSV 21F, CSH 24MF (multi-cut fodder)
- **Sweet sorghum (ethanol):** CSV 19SS, RSSV 9

### Kharif Sowing
- Time: June-July; Seed rate: 10 kg/hectare; Spacing: 45x15 cm
- Fertilizer: N:P = 80:40 kg/hectare

### Rabi Sowing (Maharashtra, Karnataka)
- Time: September-October on receding soil moisture
- Seed rate: 7.5 kg/hectare; Spacing: 45x15 cm
- Fertilizer: N:P = 50:25 kg/hectare (rainfed)

### Major Diseases
- **Grain Mold:** Discolored grains in kharif; caused by Fusarium, Aspergillus. Use resistant hybrids; avoid late harvesting
- **Charcoal Rot:** Sudden lodging at maturity; pith shredded with sclerotia. Avoid moisture stress
- **Downy Mildew:** Same as bajra — Metalaxyl seed treatment

### Major Pests
- **Shoot Fly (Atherigona soccata):** Dead heart in seedlings; MOST IMPORTANT PEST. Early sowing (within 7 days of monsoon onset); seed treatment with Imidacloprid; intercrop with 4 rows border of pearl millet
- **Stem Borer (Chilo partellus):** Same management as maize
- **Earhead Bug (Calocoris angustatus):** Milky grains get sucked dry. Spray Malathion 50% EC at 2ml/litre at milk stage

## Millets — "Nutri-Cereals" (Year of Millets 2023)

### Benefits of Millets
- Drought tolerant — grow on marginal lands with minimal inputs
- Short duration (60-90 days) — fit in multiple cropping systems
- Nutritionally superior to rice/wheat — high protein, fiber, minerals
- Low water footprint — 1/3 of rice requirement
- Gluten-free — suitable for celiac disease patients
- Low glycemic index — good for diabetes management

### Minor Millets
- **Foxtail Millet (Kangni):** Kharif crop; 70-80 days; 5 kg seed/hectare
- **Little Millet (Kutki):** Rainfed; most drought tolerant; 4 kg seed/hectare
- **Barnyard Millet (Sanwa):** Kharif; fastest growing (45-60 days); good for flood-prone areas
- **Kodo Millet (Kodon):** Rainfed; MP, Chhattisgarh; tolerates poor soils
- **Proso Millet (Cheena):** Short season; inter-crop or relay crop

### Government Support for Millets
- Included under MSP procurement
- Free distribution of HYV seeds through state programs
- Value-added product promotion under ODOP (One District One Product)
- Processing equipment subsidy under SMAM
