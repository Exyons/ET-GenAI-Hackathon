# Soybean - Comprehensive ICAR Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore
**Crop:** Soybean (Glycine max)
**Regions:** Madhya Pradesh (largest producer), Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Telangana

## Recommended Varieties
- **Madhya Pradesh:** JS 20-34, JS 20-29, JS 9560, NRC 142, NRC 150
- **Maharashtra:** MAUS 162, JS 335, MAUS 71, JS 9560
- **Rajasthan:** JS 20-34, RKS 45, JS 9560
- **Short duration (85-90 days):** JS 93-05, NRC 37 — suitable for delayed sowing

## Sowing
- **Time:** With onset of monsoon (June 20 - July 5); delayed sowing after July 15 reduces yield by 30-40%
- **Seed Rate:** 65-75 kg/hectare (depends on seed size and germination %)
- **Spacing:** 30-45 cm row spacing; 5-7 cm plant-to-plant
- **Depth:** 3-4 cm (do not sow deeper than 5 cm)
- **Seed Treatment (mandatory):**
  1. Fungicide: Carbendazim 50% WP at 2g/kg OR Thiram 75% WP at 3g/kg
  2. Insecticide: Thiamethoxam 30% FS at 10ml/kg seed
  3. Rhizobium: Bradyrhizobium japonicum culture at 5g/kg seed (apply AFTER fungicide dries; fixes atmospheric nitrogen)
  4. PSB: Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria at 5g/kg

## Fertilizer Management (per hectare)
- **N:P:K:** 20:60:40 kg/hectare — all as basal
- Soybean fixes its own nitrogen through Rhizobium — do NOT apply excess nitrogen (causes lodging and reduces nodulation)
- Sulphur: 20 kg/hectare as gypsum or bentonite sulphur (essential for oil content)
- Apply FYM/compost at 5 tonnes/hectare before sowing

## Major Diseases

### Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)
- **Symptoms:** Bright yellow patches on leaves; stunted plants; few pods with shriveled seeds
- **Vector:** Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- **Treatment:** No chemical cure for virus; control whitefly with Thiamethoxam 25% WG at 0.3g/litre
- **Prevention:** Use resistant varieties (JS 20-29, NRC 142); early sowing; avoid late monsoon planting
- **CRITICAL:** Rogue out infected plants early to prevent spread within field

### Charcoal Rot (Macrophomina phaseolina)
- **Symptoms:** Sudden wilting at pod-filling stage; stem base shows grey-black discoloration with charcoal-like sclerotia
- **Favorable conditions:** Drought stress during pod filling (September)
- **Treatment:** No effective cure; seed treatment is preventive
- **Prevention:** Irrigate during dry spells in September; crop rotation with cereals; Trichoderma enriched FYM

### Anthracnose (Colletotrichum truncatum)
- **Symptoms:** Dark brown to black sunken lesions on stems, pods, and petioles; rolled, distorted leaves
- **Treatment:** Spray Carbendazim 50% WP at 1g/litre or Thiophanate methyl 70% WP at 1g/litre at R3 (beginning pod) stage
- **Prevention:** Seed treatment; crop rotation; avoid monoculture

### Rust (Phakopsora pachyrhizi)
- **Symptoms:** Small reddish-brown pustules (uredinia) on lower leaf surface; premature defoliation
- **Favorable conditions:** High humidity, temperature 18-26°C, late-planted crop
- **Treatment:** Spray Hexaconazole 5% EC at 2ml/litre OR Propiconazole at 1ml/litre at first appearance
- **Prevention:** Early sowing; use moderately resistant varieties

### Bacterial Pustule (Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. glycines)
- **Symptoms:** Small, raised yellow-green pustules on upper leaf surface with raised center on lower surface
- **Treatment:** Spray Streptocycline 0.025% + Copper oxychloride 0.3%
- **Prevention:** Use disease-free seed; avoid overhead irrigation

## Major Pests

### Stem Fly (Melanagromyza sojae)
- **Symptoms:** Wilting and drying of seedlings; larva tunnels inside stem (visible on splitting stem)
- **Most damaging pest of soybean in India**
- **Treatment:** Seed treatment with Thiamethoxam 30% FS at 10ml/kg protects for 25-30 days. Foliar spray: Dimethoate 30% EC at 1.5ml/litre at 20-25 DAS
- **ETL:** 10% plants infested

### Girdle Beetle (Obereopsis brevis)
- **Symptoms:** Girdling rings on stem and petioles; affected parts break and hang
- **Peak:** August-September
- **Treatment:** Spray Triazophos 40% EC at 1.5ml/litre
- **IPM:** Hand collection and destruction of girdled plant parts

### Tobacco Caterpillar (Spodoptera litura)
- **Symptoms:** Irregular large holes in leaves; gregarious feeding (young larvae); complete defoliation in severe cases
- **Treatment:** Spray Flubendiamide 20% WG at 0.25g/litre OR Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC at 0.3ml/litre
- **IPM:** Pheromone traps (Spodolure) at 5/hectare; collect and destroy egg masses; NSKE 5% spray at 15-day intervals
- **Bio-control:** NPV (Nucleopolyhedrovirus) of S. litura at 250 LE/hectare in evening hours

### Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing, honeydew, transmits YMV
- **Treatment:** Spray Spiromesifen 22.9% SC at 0.7ml/litre
- **ETL:** 5 adults per trifoliate

### Green Semilooper (Chrysodeixis acuta)
- **Symptoms:** Irregular feeding holes; larvae move in looping fashion
- **Treatment:** Spray Quinalphos 25% EC at 2ml/litre or Emamectin benzoate at 0.4g/litre
- **ETL:** 4 larvae per metre row

## Weed Management
- **Pre-emergence:** Pendimethalin 30% EC at 3.3 litre/hectare OR Diclosulam 84% WDG at 26g/hectare within 3 DAS
- **Post-emergence:** Imazethapyr 10% SL at 750 ml/hectare at 20-25 DAS (controls both grassy and broad-leaved weeds)
- One hand weeding/hoeing at 30-35 DAS is highly beneficial
- **Do NOT use Atrazine or 2,4-D on soybean — they will kill the crop**

## Harvest
- Harvest when 95% of pods turn brown and leaves have shed
- Moisture content: 13-14% at harvest for safe storage
- Use combine with soybean settings (low cylinder speed: 400-500 rpm; wider concave clearance)
- Delay causes pod shattering and grain loss (up to 20% loss in some varieties)
- Dry to 10% moisture for safe storage; use clean jute bags or metal bins
