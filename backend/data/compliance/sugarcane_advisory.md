# Sugarcane - Comprehensive ICAR Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Sugarcane Breeding Institute (SBI), Coimbatore; Indian Institute of Sugarcane Research (IISR), Lucknow
**Crop:** Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)
**Regions:** Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Bihar, Punjab, Haryana, Uttarakhand

## Recommended Varieties

### Subtropical Zone (UP, Bihar, Punjab, Haryana)
- **Early maturing (10-11 months):** CoLk 94184, CoS 8436, CoS 767, BO 91
- **Mid-late maturing (12-14 months):** Co 0238 (most popular), CoLk 11206, CoS 13235
- **Waterlogged areas:** CoS 767, CoLk 8102

### Tropical Zone (Maharashtra, Karnataka, TN, AP)
- **Early maturing:** Co 86032, CoC 671, CoV 92102
- **Mid-late maturing:** Co 0238, Co 11015, CoM 265

### Red Rot Resistant
- Co 0238, CoLk 94184, CoS 8436 — critical for UP/Bihar

## Planting
- **Autumn planting (best):** October — higher yield, avoids red rot
- **Spring planting:** February-March (most common in subtropical India)
- **Adsali planting (tropical):** July-August — 16-18 month crop, highest yield
- **Sett rate:** 40,000-45,000 three-budded setts/hectare (6-8 tonnes setts)
- **Sett treatment:** Dip in Carbendazim 0.1% for 15 minutes (prevents smut and red rot); then Chlorpyrifos 0.04% for 10 minutes (prevents termites/borers)
- **Spacing:** 75-90 cm row spacing; trench planting at 120-150 cm recommended for better yield
- **Planting method:** Horizontal flat planting in furrows; ring method for ratoon management

## Fertilizer Management (per hectare)
| Nutrient | Plant crop | Ratoon crop |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Nitrogen | 300 kg | 375 kg |
| P2O5 | 100 kg | 100 kg |
| K2O | 120 kg | 120 kg |

- Apply full P and K + 1/3 N as basal
- Remaining N in 2 equal splits at 45 and 90 DAP
- **Bio-fertilizers:** Inoculate setts with Acetobacter diazotrophicus + Phosphobacteria (saves 25% N and 25% P)
- Apply Sulphur at 45 kg/hectare through gypsum for better juice quality
- Apply FeSO4 at 25 kg/hectare if iron chlorosis (yellowing) appears

## Intercropping
- **Recommended intercrops:** Potato, wheat, mustard, lentil, coriander, garlic (planted between rows)
- **Period:** First 90-120 days before canopy closes
- **Benefit:** Additional income + ground cover reduces weed pressure
- **Do NOT intercrop with:** Crops that compete for light or nutrients during grand growth phase

## Water Management
- **Total requirement:** 1500-2000 mm over crop duration
- **Critical stages:** Germination (30 DAS), Tillering (45-120 DAS), Grand Growth (120-270 DAS)
- **Stress tolerance:** Relatively drought tolerant in maturity phase; water stress during grand growth reduces yield drastically
- **Drip irrigation:** 30-40% water saving; sub-surface drip most efficient
- **Waterlogging:** Drain within 48 hours — prolonged waterlogging causes red rot and poor root development

## Major Diseases

### Red Rot (Colletotrichum falcatum)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing and drying of leaves from top; reddening of internal cane tissue with white transverse bands on splitting; foul smell from affected cane
- **Most destructive disease of sugarcane in India**
- **Favorable conditions:** Waterlogged soils, wounded cane, rainy season
- **Treatment:** No effective chemical cure in field. Remove and burn infected clumps. Flood field to kill pathogen spores
- **Prevention:** Use resistant varieties (Co 0238); healthy sett selection; sett treatment with Carbendazim; avoid waterlogging; crop rotation with paddy
- **CRITICAL WARNING:** Do NOT use sets from red rot infected fields for planting

### Smut (Sporisorium scitamineum)
- **Symptoms:** Black whip-like structure emerging from growing point; profuse tillering of thin canes
- **Treatment:** Remove and burn smut whips before they open and release spores
- **Prevention:** Sett treatment (hot water 50°C for 2 hours + Carbendazim dip); use resistant varieties

### Wilt (Fusarium sacchari)
- **Symptoms:** Gradual yellowing and drying from 5th-6th month; pith becomes hollow; purple/brown discoloration of vascular bundles
- **Treatment:** Remove infected stools; drench soil with Carbendazim 0.1%
- **Prevention:** Use resistant varieties; avoid ratooning wilt-prone fields

### Grassy Shoot Disease (Phytoplasma)
- **Symptoms:** Profuse tillering with thin, grassy shoots; white or pale green chlorotic leaves; no cane formation
- **Vector:** Leafhopper (Proutista moesta)
- **Treatment:** No cure. Rogue out infected plants immediately
- **Prevention:** Use disease-free seed cane; control leafhopper vector; tissue culture raised plantlets

## Major Pests

### Early Shoot Borer (Chilo infuscatellus)
- **Symptoms:** Dead heart in young shoots; bore holes with frass at ground level
- **Peak:** March-June (during germination/early tillering)
- **Treatment:** Apply Carbofuran 3G at 40 kg/hectare in furrows at planting; spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC at 0.3ml/litre
- **IPM:** Light traps to monitor moth activity; remove dead hearts to prevent spread
- **ETL:** 10% dead hearts

### Top Borer (Scirpophaga excerptalis)
- **Symptoms:** Dead heart with parallel rows of shot holes on unfurling leaves; bunchy top appearance
- **Treatment:** Spray Emamectin benzoate 5% SG at 0.4g/litre
- **IPM:** Release Trichogramma chilonis at 50,000/hectare at monthly intervals (4-5 releases)

### Internode Borer (Chilo sacchariphagus indicus)
- **Symptoms:** Bore holes at internodes; frass visible; reduces cane weight and sugar recovery
- **Treatment:** Spray Fipronil 5% SC at 2ml/litre directed at cane
- **IPM:** Remove water shoots (which harbor larvae); trash mulching

### Termites (Odontotermes obesus)
- **Symptoms:** Drying of cane from base; roots eaten; soil sheeting on cane
- **Treatment:** Soil application of Chlorpyrifos 20% EC at 5 litre/hectare with irrigation water
- **Prevention:** Use well-decomposed FYM (not raw); sett treatment; regular irrigation prevents buildup

### Woolly Aphid (Ceratovacuna lanigera)
- **Symptoms:** White woolly colonies on underside of leaves; honeydew and sooty mold; leaf drying in severe cases
- **Treatment:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.3ml/litre
- **Bio-control:** Release Dipha aphidivora (predator) at 250 adults/hectare — very effective

### White Grub (Holotrichia spp.)
- **Symptoms:** Patchy yellowing; uprooted plants reveal root damage; grubs in soil
- **Treatment:** Soil application of Imidacloprid 0.5G at 20 kg/hectare
- **IPM:** Light traps for adult beetles (May-June); deep ploughing to expose grubs

## Ratoon Management
- **First ratoon:** 75-80% yield of plant crop; second ratoon: 60-65% — do not take more than 2 ratoons
- Immediately after harvest: cut stubbles close to ground level, fill gaps with setts, apply 25% extra nitrogen
- Off-bar (intercultivation) at 15 days to break old roots and encourage new tillers
- Apply Propiconazole 25% EC at 1ml/litre spray if leaf diseases appear in ratoon

## Harvest
- Harvest at peak maturity: test Brix with hand refractometer (>18°Brix in top and bottom internodes)
- Cut at ground level — buried internodes have high sugar
- Remove tops (used as fodder) — do not send to mill
- Transport to mill within 24 hours — sugar loss is 0.05%/hour of delay
- **Jaggery making:** Harvest at same maturity; crush within 12 hours; boil juice in open pans
