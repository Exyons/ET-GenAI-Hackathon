# Cotton - Comprehensive ICAR Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur
**Crop:** Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / G. arboreum)
**Regions:** Maharashtra, Gujarat, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Punjab, Haryana

## Recommended Varieties

### Bt Cotton Hybrids (most common)
- Follow state agricultural university recommendations for your zone
- Ensure BG-II refuge: Plant 5 rows of non-Bt cotton (desi or pigeon pea) for every 20 rows of Bt cotton

### Non-Bt / Desi Varieties
- **Maharashtra:** PKV Rajat, AKA-8, PA 183
- **Gujarat:** G.Cot 23, Gujarat Cotton 25
- **Organic farming:** Suraj, PKV 081, Jayadhar (naturally pest-tolerant desi varieties)

## Sowing
- **Time:** After onset of monsoon (June-July); dry sowing 15 days before expected monsoon is also practiced
- **Spacing:** 90x60 cm (irrigated) or 90x45 cm (rainfed); wider spacing in heavy rainfall areas
- **Seed Rate:** 2.5-3 kg/hectare (Bt hybrids); 10-12 kg/hectare (desi varieties)
- **Seed Treatment:** Imidacloprid 70% WS at 5g/kg seed (protects against early sucking pests for 30-40 days)
- **Depth:** 3-5 cm

## Fertilizer Management (per hectare, irrigated)
| Stage | N (kg) | P2O5 (kg) | K2O (kg) |
|-------|--------|-----------|----------|
| Basal | 30 | 60 | 60 |
| 30 DAS | 30 | - | - |
| 60 DAS | 30 | - | - |
| 90 DAS (Square/Flowering) | 30 | - | - |

- **Total NPK:** 120:60:60 kg/hectare (irrigated); 60:30:30 (rainfed)
- Magnesium deficiency (reddening): Apply MgSO4 25 kg/hectare as basal
- Boron deficiency (poor boll setting): Foliar spray Borax 0.2% at flowering

## Water Management
- Cotton is moderately drought tolerant but needs moisture at critical stages
- **Critical irrigations:** Flowering, boll development
- **Drip irrigation:** 40% water saving; 20-30% yield increase. Recommended spacing: laterals at 1.2m, drippers at 60cm

## Major Diseases

### Bacterial Blight (Xanthomonas citri pv. malvacearum)
- **Symptoms:** Angular water-soaked spots on leaves that turn brown; blackarm on stems and petioles; boll rot in severe cases
- **Favorable conditions:** Heavy rain, high humidity, temperature 25-35°C
- **Treatment:** Spray Streptocycline 0.01% + Copper oxychloride 0.3% at first appearance; repeat after 10-15 days
- **Prevention:** Use disease-free seed; delint seeds with H2SO4 (concentrated) for 2 min; resistant varieties

### Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. vasinfectum)
- **Symptoms:** Wilting of plants from one side; browning of vascular tissue visible on cutting stem longitudinally
- **Treatment:** No effective chemical cure. Remove and destroy infected plants
- **Prevention:** Use resistant varieties; crop rotation with cereals for 3 years; seed treatment with Trichoderma viride at 10g/kg

### Grey Mildew (Ramularia areola)
- **Symptoms:** Angular powdery grey/white spots on lower leaf surface; appears in Oct-Nov when nights become cooler
- **Treatment:** Spray Carbendazim 50% WP at 1g/litre OR Copper hydroxide 77% WP at 2g/litre
- **Prevention:** Wider spacing for air circulation; avoid late sowing

### Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV)
- **Symptoms:** Upward or downward leaf curling, vein thickening, enation (outgrowths) on underside of leaves; stunted plants
- **Vector:** Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- **Treatment:** No direct cure; control whitefly vector aggressively
- **Management:** Early sowing (April-May in Punjab/Haryana); rogue out infected plants early; resistant varieties (HD 432, LHH 144)

## Major Pests

### American Bollworm (Helicoverpa armigera)
- **Symptoms:** Bore holes in squares, flowers, and bolls; frass at entry point
- **Treatment:** Spray Emamectin benzoate 5% SG at 0.4g/litre OR Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC at 0.3ml/litre
- **ETL:** 1 larva per plant or 5-10% damaged squares/bolls
- **IPM:** Install pheromone traps (Helilure) at 5/hectare; release Trichogramma chilonis egg parasitoids at 1.5 lakh/hectare, 6-8 releases from squaring

### Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella)
- **Symptoms:** Rosette flowering (petals fused); larvae inside green/open bolls; exit holes in bolls
- **Treatment:** Spray Profenofos 50% EC at 2ml/litre
- **IPM:** Pheromone traps with gossyplure at 5/hectare; mating disruption with 40 dispensers/hectare
- **Critical post-harvest:** Destroy crop residues by November; do not keep cotton stalks in field — they harbor pupae

### Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing, honeydew, sooty mold, transmits CLCuV
- **Treatment:** Spray Spiromesifen 22.9% SC at 0.7ml/litre OR Diafenthiuron 50% WP at 1g/litre
- **ETL:** 5-8 adults per leaf (top 3 leaves)
- **Warning:** Avoid pyrethroids — they cause whitefly resurgence
- **Cultural control:** Remove alternate hosts (weeds) like Parthenium; avoid continuous cotton

### Aphids (Aphis gossypii)
- **Symptoms:** Curling of young leaves; honeydew; black sooty mold
- **Treatment:** Spray Flonicamid 50% WG at 0.3g/litre OR Acetamiprid 20% SP at 0.2g/litre
- **Bio-control:** Conserve lady beetles and Chrysoperla carnea

### Jassids / Leafhoppers (Amrasca biguttula)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing and curling of leaf margins (hopper burn); V-shaped yellowing
- **Treatment:** Spray Fipronil 5% SC at 2ml/litre OR Buprofezin 25% SC at 2ml/litre
- **ETL:** 2 nymphs per leaf (on 3rd leaf from top)

### Thrips (Thrips tabaci)
- **Symptoms:** Silvery patches on leaves; upward curling of leaves in seedling stage
- **Treatment:** Spray Fipronil 5% SC at 2ml/litre

### Mealybug (Phenacoccus solenopsis)
- **Symptoms:** White cottony masses on stem, leaves; stunting; sooty mold
- **Treatment:** Spray Profenofos 50% EC at 2ml/litre (drenching spray needed to penetrate waxy covering)
- **Bio-control:** Release parasitoid Aenasius bambawalei

## Weed Management
- Pre-emergence: Pendimethalin 30% EC at 3.3 litre/hectare
- Post-emergence: Pyrithiobac sodium 10% EC at 625 ml/hectare (selective in cotton)
- Intercultivation at 30 and 60 DAS
- Mulching with crop residues or polyethylene mulch (drip irrigation system)

## Picking and Marketing
- Pick cotton in 3-4 rounds at 15-day intervals when bolls fully open
- Pick in dry weather; avoid picking wet cotton (grade loss)
- Maintain trash content below 5% for better market price
- Grade separately: first picking usually has best quality (longer staple)
- Do not mix different variety cottons

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Calendar
1. **Sowing-30 DAS:** Seed treatment protects against sucking pests; install yellow sticky traps
2. **30-60 DAS:** Release Chrysoperla at 10,000/hectare; Trichogramma releases begin
3. **60-90 DAS:** Monitor bollworm with pheromone traps; spray NSKE 5% at 10-day intervals
4. **90-120 DAS:** Need-based chemical sprays; prioritize bio-rational insecticides
5. **Post-harvest:** Destroy stalks; deep plough; break pest cycle
