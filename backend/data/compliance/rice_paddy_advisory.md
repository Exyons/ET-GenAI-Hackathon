# Rice (Paddy) - Comprehensive ICAR Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Indian Institute of Rice Research (IIRR), Hyderabad
**Crop:** Rice (Oryza sativa)
**Regions:** All India — major states: West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Assam, Karnataka

## Varieties Recommended by Region

### Kharif Season (June-November)
- **Punjab/Haryana:** Pusa Basmati 1121, Pusa Basmati 1509, PR 126, PR 127
- **Uttar Pradesh:** Pusa 44, Sarjoo 52, NDR 359, Swarna
- **West Bengal:** Swarna, MTU 7029, IET 4786, Khitish
- **Andhra Pradesh/Telangana:** BPT 5204 (Samba Mahsuri), MTU 1010, RNR 15048
- **Tamil Nadu:** ADT 43, CO 51, BPT 5204, White Ponni
- **Odisha:** Swarna, Lalat, Khandagiri, Naveen
- **Bihar:** Rajendra Sweta, Rajshree, Swarna Sub-1

### Rabi Season (November-March)
- Short-duration varieties: Pusa 1460, ADT 37, Krishna Hamsa

## Nursery Management
1. **Seed Rate:** 30-40 kg/hectare for transplanting; 80-100 kg/hectare for direct seeding
2. **Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with Carbendazim 50% WP at 2g/kg seed OR Trichoderma viride at 10g/kg seed to prevent seed-borne diseases
3. **Nursery Area:** 1000 sq.m nursery for transplanting 1 hectare
4. **Sowing:** Sow pre-germinated seeds on puddled nursery beds. Maintain 2-3 cm water depth

## Land Preparation
- Plough 2-3 times and puddle the field thoroughly
- Level the field using laser land leveler for uniform water distribution
- Incorporate 10-12 tonnes/hectare of well-decomposed FYM before last puddling

## Transplanting
- **Age of Seedlings:** 20-25 days for short duration; 25-30 days for medium duration
- **Spacing:** 20x15 cm for high-yielding varieties; 20x10 cm for Basmati
- **Depth:** 2-3 cm only; do not plant too deep
- **Seedlings per hill:** 2-3 seedlings

## Fertilizer Management (per hectare)
| Nutrient | Basal | 25 DAT | 50 DAT | Panicle Initiation |
|----------|-------|--------|--------|-------------------|
| N (Urea) | 40 kg | 40 kg | 20 kg | 20 kg |
| P (SSP) | 60 kg | - | - | - |
| K (MOP) | 40 kg | - | 20 kg | - |
| Zinc Sulphate | 25 kg | - | - | - |

**Note:** Apply N in 4 splits. Zinc deficiency is very common in rice — always apply ZnSO4 as basal.

## Water Management
- Maintain 5 cm standing water from transplanting to tillering
- Drain field at maximum tillering stage for 3-4 days
- Maintain 5 cm water during panicle initiation to flowering
- Drain field 15 days before harvest

## Major Diseases

### Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)
- **Symptoms:** Diamond-shaped spots with grey center on leaves; neck blast causes broken panicles
- **Favorable conditions:** Temperature 25-28°C, high humidity, excessive nitrogen
- **Treatment:** Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP at 0.6g/litre OR Isoprothiolane 40% EC at 1.5ml/litre
- **Prevention:** Avoid excessive nitrogen; use resistant varieties; balanced fertilization

### Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae)
- **Symptoms:** Water-soaked lesions on leaf margins turning yellow to white; V-shaped wilting from leaf tip
- **Favorable conditions:** Heavy rain, flooding, high humidity, clipping of leaf tips during transplanting
- **Treatment:** Spray Streptocycline 0.015% + Copper oxychloride 0.25%. Drain excess water
- **Prevention:** Use certified disease-free seeds; avoid clipping seedling tips; balanced N application

### Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani)
- **Symptoms:** Oval/irregular greenish-grey lesions on leaf sheath near water level
- **Treatment:** Spray Hexaconazole 5% EC at 2ml/litre OR Validamycin 3% SL at 2ml/litre
- **Prevention:** Wider spacing; avoid excessive nitrogen; remove stubble from previous crop

### Brown Spot (Bipolaris oryzae)
- **Symptoms:** Oval brown spots on leaves with yellow halo; indicates poor soil nutrition
- **Treatment:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5g/litre
- **Prevention:** Balanced nutrition especially potassium; seed treatment with Carbendazim

## Major Pests

### Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas)
- **Symptoms:** Dead heart in vegetative stage; white ear head in reproductive stage
- **Treatment:** Apply Cartap hydrochloride 4G at 25 kg/hectare in standing water OR spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC at 0.3ml/litre
- **ETL:** 5% dead hearts or 2% white ear heads

### Brown Plant Hopper (Nilaparvata lugens)
- **Symptoms:** Circular drying patches ("hopper burn"); honeydew deposits on lower parts
- **Treatment:** Spray Pymetrozine 50% WG at 0.6g/litre OR Dinotefuran 20% SG at 0.5g/litre. Direct spray at base of plant
- **Warning:** Do NOT use synthetic pyrethroids — they cause BPH resurgence
- **Prevention:** Avoid excessive nitrogen; drain water periodically; grow resistant varieties

### Leaf Folder (Cnaphalocrocis medinalis)
- **Symptoms:** Longitudinal folding of leaves with scraping of green matter
- **Treatment:** Spray Flubendiamide 20% WG at 0.25g/litre
- **ETL:** 10% damaged leaves

### Gall Midge (Orseolia oryzae)
- **Symptoms:** Silver shoot (onion leaf-like tubular gall) during vegetative stage
- **Treatment:** Apply Fipronil 0.3G at 25 kg/hectare
- **Prevention:** Early planting; use resistant varieties (Suraksha, Abhaya)

## Weed Management
- Apply Pretilachlor 50% EC at 1 litre/hectare within 3 days of transplanting
- For broad-leaved weeds: Bispyribac sodium 10% SC at 200 ml/hectare at 20-25 DAT
- One hand weeding at 30 DAT is recommended in addition to herbicide

## Harvest and Post-Harvest
- Harvest when 80-85% of grains turn golden yellow
- Moisture content should be 20-22% at harvest
- Dry paddy to 14% moisture for safe storage
- Store in clean, dry godowns; fumigate with Aluminium phosphide tablets (3g/tonne)

## Organic Rice Production (NPOF Guidelines)
- Use Azolla as biofertilizer (dual crop): Inoculate at 500 kg/hectare fresh Azolla
- Apply Neem cake at 500 kg/hectare to control soil pests
- Spray Neem oil 5% or NSKE 5% for pest management
- Pheromone traps for stem borer: Install 5 traps/hectare
