# Government Agricultural Schemes - Advisory for Indian Farmers

**Source:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
**Updated:** 2024-25

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Benefit:** Rs 6,000/year in 3 installments of Rs 2,000 each
- **Eligibility:** All landholding farmer families
- **Exclusion:** Institutional landholders, income tax payers, government employees
- **How to apply:** Through local Common Service Centre (CSC) or pmkisan.gov.in
- **Documents:** Aadhaar card, bank account, land records
- **Status check:** pmkisan.gov.in → Beneficiary Status

## PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Benefit:** Crop insurance against natural calamities, pests, diseases
- **Premium:** Kharif: 2% of sum insured; Rabi: 1.5%; Commercial/Horticultural: 5%
- **Remaining premium paid by government**
- **Crops covered:** All food and oilseed crops + annual commercial/horticultural crops
- **How to apply:** Through bank (if loanee farmer, auto-enrolled) or CSC or pmfby.gov.in
- **Claim filing:** Report crop loss within 72 hours to insurance company or via Crop Insurance App
- **CRITICAL:** Intimation within 72 hours is MANDATORY for individual claims

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- **Benefit:** Short-term credit at 7% interest (effectively 4% with subsidy for timely repayment)
- **Credit limit:** Based on land holding, crop pattern, and scale of finance
- **Also covers:** Allied activities (dairy, fishery, animal husbandry)
- **How to apply:** Any scheduled commercial bank, cooperative bank, or RRB
- **Documents:** Land records, identity proof, passport photo
- **Personal accident insurance:** Rs 50,000 death, Rs 25,000 disability (included)
- **Repayment:** Within 12 months for short-term; flexible for investment credit

## Soil Health Card Scheme
- **Benefit:** Free soil testing and nutrient-based recommendations every 2 years
- **How to get:** Contact nearest KVK, district agriculture office, or state soil testing lab
- **Parameters tested:** N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, Organic Carbon
- **Online portal:** soilhealth.dac.gov.in

## PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana (PMKSY)
- **Benefit:** Subsidy for micro-irrigation (drip, sprinkler)
- **Subsidy:** Small/Marginal farmers: 55% for drip, 55% for sprinkler; Other farmers: 45%
- **How to apply:** State agriculture/horticulture department or pmksy.gov.in
- **Coverage:** Equipment cost + installation
- **Impact:** 40-50% water saving, 20-30% yield increase with drip irrigation

## National Mission on Oilseeds and Oil Palm (NMOOP)
- **Benefit:** Subsidy for oilseed production inputs, oil palm planting
- **Components:** Improved seed distribution, IPM demonstrations, oil palm area expansion
- **Oil Palm:** Rs 29,000/hectare for planting + maintenance for 4 years
- **Contact:** State agriculture department

## Sub Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)
- **Benefit:** 40-50% subsidy on agricultural machinery
- **Machinery:** Tractors, power tillers, rotavators, seed drills, combine harvesters, sprayers
- **Custom Hiring Centres:** Farmers can rent machinery at subsidized rates
- **How to apply:** agrimachinery.nic.in or state agriculture department
- **SC/ST/Women farmers:** Get additional 10% subsidy

## National Horticulture Mission (NHM/MIDH)
- **Benefit:** Subsidy for fruits, vegetables, spices, flowers, medicinal plants
- **Components:**
  - Planting material: 50-75% subsidy
  - Protected cultivation (polyhouse/shade net): 50% up to Rs 56 lakhs
  - Organic farming: Rs 10,000/hectare for 3 years
  - Post-harvest infrastructure: 35-50% subsidy for cold storage, pack houses, ripening chambers
  - Integrated pest management: Subsidy for bio-control labs, IPM demonstrations
- **How to apply:** midh.gov.in or state horticulture department

## Agricultural Marketing

### e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
- **Purpose:** Online trading of agricultural commodities
- **Benefit:** Transparent price discovery; access to buyers across India; electronic payment
- **Registration:** Through local APMC/mandi where e-NAM is enabled
- **Portal:** enam.gov.in
- **Currently:** 1361 mandis across India integrated

### Minimum Support Price (MSP)
- Government declares MSP for 23 crops each season
- **Kharif MSP crops:** Rice, jowar, bajra, maize, ragi, tur/arhar, moong, urad, groundnut, sunflower, soybean, sesamum, nigerseed, cotton, jute
- **Rabi MSP crops:** Wheat, barley, gram/chana, masur, rapeseed/mustard, safflower, copra
- **Procurement:** Through government agencies (FCI for wheat/rice, NAFED for oilseeds/pulses)
- **Where to sell at MSP:** Government procurement centres set up during harvest season

## Emergency/Disaster Support

### PM Kisan Bima Yojana for Cattle
- **Benefit:** Insurance for cattle against death from disease/accident
- **Premium:** 50% subsidy for crossbred cattle; higher for indigenous breeds
- **Sum insured:** Based on market value of animal

### Natural Disaster Compensation (SDRF/NDRF)
- **Crop loss >33%:** Eligible for compensation
- **Rates:** Rs 6,800/hectare for rainfed crops; Rs 13,500/hectare for irrigated crops; Rs 18,000/hectare for perennial crops
- **How to claim:** Report to Patwari/Village Revenue Officer → Gram Sabah → Block → District → State
- **Deadline:** Report within 15 days of calamity
