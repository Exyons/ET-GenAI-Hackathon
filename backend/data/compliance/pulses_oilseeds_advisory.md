# Pulses & Oilseeds - ICAR Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR), Kanpur; ICAR-Indian Institute of Oilseeds Research (IIOR), Hyderabad; ICAR-Directorate of Groundnut Research (DGR), Junagadh
**Crops:** Chickpea, Pigeon Pea, Moong, Urad, Groundnut, Mustard, Sunflower
**Regions:** All India

---

## Chickpea / Chana (Cicer arietinum)

### Varieties
- **Desi (brown):** Pusa 256, JG 11, JG 14, JAKI 9218, GNG 1958, Vijay
- **Kabuli (white, large-seeded):** Pusa 1108, KAK 2, IPCK 2004-29, L 552
- **Wilt resistant:** JG 11, Digvijay, Vishal, Phule G 0517

### Sowing
- Time: October 15 - November 15 (delayed sowing reduces yield sharply)
- Seed rate: Desi: 75-80 kg/hectare; Kabuli: 100 kg/hectare
- Spacing: 30x10 cm
- Seed treatment: Carbendazim 2g/kg + Rhizobium + PSB

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- N:P = 20:40 kg as basal (chickpea fixes its own N through Rhizobium — excess N is harmful)
- Sulphur: 20 kg/hectare through gypsum

### Major Diseases
- **Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. ciceris):** Yellowing, drooping, drying of plant from one side or completely. MOST DESTRUCTIVE. Use wilt-resistant varieties (JG 11); seed treatment with Trichoderma viride at 10g/kg + Carbendazim at 2g/kg; crop rotation (3 years)
- **Ascochyta Blight:** Circular brown spots with concentric rings on leaves, stems, pods. Spray Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre; avoid overhead irrigation
- **Dry Root Rot (Rhizoctonia bataticola):** Dry rotting of roots; plant dies at pod-filling. No chemical cure; avoid moisture stress; Trichoderma-enriched FYM
- **Botrytis Grey Mold:** Grey fuzzy growth on leaves/flowers in foggy weather. Spray Carbendazim at 1g/litre; wider spacing

### Major Pests
- **Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera):** MOST DAMAGING; larvae bore into pods and eat developing grains. Spray Emamectin benzoate at 0.4g/litre or Chlorantraniliprole at 0.3ml/litre. IPM: Bird perches (50/hectare); pheromone traps; NSKE 5% spray; Bt spray; NPV at 250 LE/hectare
- **ETL:** 1 larva per metre row or 2 larvae per plant
- **Cutworm:** Cuts seedlings at ground level at night. Chlorpyrifos 20% EC at 2ml/litre soil drench at base

### Harvest
- Harvest when 80% of pods turn brown and plant starts drying
- Moisture: 18-20% at harvest; dry to 10% for storage
- Store in clean bags; use Neem leaves or dried neem powder to prevent pulse beetle infestation

## Pigeon Pea / Tur / Arhar (Cajanus cajan)

### Varieties
- **Short duration (120-140 days):** ICPL 87, Pusa 992, UPAS 120
- **Medium duration (160-180 days):** Asha (ICPL 87119), Maruti, BDN 711
- **Long duration (200-270 days):** Bahar, NDA 1, MA 6

### Sowing
- Time: June 15 - July 15 (kharif)
- Seed rate: 12-15 kg/hectare
- Spacing: 60-75 x 20-25 cm
- Intercrop: Pigeon pea + Soybean (2:4 rows) is highly recommended in MP/Maharashtra

### Major Diseases
- **Fusarium Wilt:** Same as chickpea; use resistant varieties (Asha, BDN 711)
- **Sterility Mosaic Disease (SMD):** Small, pale leaves clustered at top; no flowering. Vector: Eriophyid mite. Use resistant varieties; destroy infected plants; Wettable Sulphur 3g/litre spray
- **Phytophthora Blight:** Waterlogging causes stem rot and death. Ridge planting; drainage; Metalaxyl + Mancozeb spray

### Major Pests
- **Pod Borer (Helicoverpa):** Same as chickpea; most critical during Oct-Dec flowering/podding
- **Pod Fly (Melanagromyza obtusa):** Maggot feeds inside pod; exit holes visible. Spray Triazophos 40% EC at 1.5ml/litre at 50% flowering
- **Maruca Pod Borer:** Webbing of flowers and pods. Spray Flubendiamide at 0.25g/litre

## Green Gram / Moong (Vigna radiata) & Black Gram / Urad (Vigna mungo)

### Varieties
- **Moong:** Pusa Vishal, IPM 02-3, SML 668, MH 421 (summer); TM 96-2 (MYMV resistant)
- **Urad:** Pant U-31, IPU 2-43, KU 301, Uttara (MYMV resistant)

### Sowing
- **Kharif:** July; **Spring/Summer:** March-April
- Seed rate: 20-25 kg/hectare; Spacing: 30x10 cm
- SHORT DURATION: 60-70 days — excellent for intensive cropping systems

### Major Disease
- **Mung/Urad Yellow Mosaic Virus (MYMV/UYMV):** Bright yellow mosaic on leaves; severe stunting. Vector: Whitefly. **MOST IMPORTANT DISEASE.** Use resistant varieties; control whitefly; early sowing; rogue infected plants

### Major Pest
- **Whitefly:** Vector of MYMV. Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG at 0.3g/litre
- **Pod borer complex:** Same management as chickpea/pigeon pea

## Groundnut / Peanut (Arachis hypogaea)

### Varieties
- **Bunch type (kharif):** Kadiri 6, TAG 24, TG 37A, GPBD 4
- **Semi-spreading:** ICGS 76, JL 501, TG 51
- **Spanish type (summer):** GG 20, TG 26, Phule Pragati

### Sowing
- Kharif: June-July; Summer: January-March (irrigated)
- Seed rate: 80-100 kg/hectare (kernels); pre-treat with Trichoderma + Rhizobium
- Spacing: 30x10 cm (bunch); 45x10 cm (spreading)

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- N:P:K = 20:40:40 kg (legume — low N needed)
- Gypsum: 500 kg/hectare at flowering for calcium (CRITICAL for pod filling)
- Boron: Borax 10 kg/hectare as basal

### Major Diseases
- **Tikka Disease / Leaf Spot (Cercospora spp.):** Brown circular spots on leaves; defoliation. Spray Chlorothalonil 75% WP at 2g/litre or Carbendazim at 1g/litre
- **Collar Rot / Stem Rot (Sclerotium rolfsii):** White mycelial fan at collar region; sudden death. Seed treatment with Trichoderma viride; avoid waterlogging
- **Aflatoxin Contamination (Aspergillus flavus):** Invisible in field; toxic mold on kernels; major export rejection issue. **CRITICAL for food safety.** Harvest on time; dry quickly to <8% moisture; sort out damaged/discolored pods; store in clean dry conditions
- **Bud Necrosis (Groundnut Bud Necrosis Virus):** Ring spots on leaves; death of terminal bud. Vector: Thrips. Use resistant varieties (ICGS 76); control thrips early

### Major Pests
- **White Grub (Holotrichia spp.):** Most destructive in sandy soils (Rajasthan, Gujarat). Soil application of Chlorpyrifos 10G; light traps for adult beetles in May-June
- **Leaf Miner:** Blotch mines on leaves. Usually doesn't need treatment unless severe
- **Spodoptera:** Defoliation. Same management as soybean

## Mustard / Rapeseed (Brassica juncea)

### Varieties
- **Rajasthan:** RH 749, NRCHB 101, Pusa Bold, Varuna
- **UP/MP:** Pusa Mustard 25, Pusa Mustard 28, NRCDR 2, Urvashi
- **Eastern India:** B 9, Vardan, BARI Sarisha 14

### Sowing
- Time: October 10-25 (timely); delayed sowing after November 10 reduces yield
- Seed rate: 5 kg/hectare (thinning at 15 DAS)
- Spacing: 30-45 x 10-15 cm
- Seed treatment: Metalaxyl at 6g/kg (for white rust and downy mildew)

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- N:P:K:S = 80:40:20:40 kg
- **Sulphur is CRITICAL** for oil content — apply 40 kg/hectare as elemental sulphur or gypsum
- Boron: Borax 10 kg/hectare or foliar spray of Borax 0.2% at flowering

### Major Diseases
- **Alternaria Blight:** Dark spots on leaves, stems, pods; most common disease. Spray Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre starting from 45 DAS; 2-3 sprays at 15-day intervals
- **White Rust (Albugo candida):** White pustules on underside of leaves; distorted inflorescence (stag head). Spray Metalaxyl + Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre at first appearance
- **Sclerotinia Rot:** White cottony growth on stem at branching point; stem breaks. Spray Carbendazim at 1g/litre. Crop rotation important

### Major Pests
- **Mustard Aphid (Lipaphis erysimi):** MOST DAMAGING. Colonies on inflorescence and pods; honeydew; stunted pods. Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.3ml/litre at first appearance. **Critical window:** January-February when temperatures rise
- **Painted Bug (Bagrada hilaris):** Sucks sap from seedlings. Spray Malathion 50% EC at 2ml/litre
- **Sawfly (Athalia lugens proxima):** Larvae eat leaves entirely. Hand collection of larvae; spray Malathion

### Bee Pollination
- Mustard is highly cross-pollinated by bees
- Keep 5-8 honeybee colonies/hectare during flowering
- **AVOID insecticide sprays during flowering** — kills pollinators
- Spray insecticides in evening only if essential (after bees return to hive)

## Sunflower (Helianthus annuus)

### Varieties
- **Hybrids:** KBSH 44, DRSH 1, LSFH 171, RSFH 130
- **High oleic:** Helps command premium price

### Sowing
- Kharif: June-July; Rabi: October; Spring: January-February
- Seed rate: 5-6 kg/hectare
- Spacing: 60x30 cm
- **Bee pollination essential:** Keep 5 colonies/hectare at 10% flowering

### Major Issues
- **Alternaria Leaf Blight:** Spray Mancozeb
- **Sclerotinia head rot:** Most important in kharif. Avoid dense planting; drainage
- **Head borer (Helicoverpa):** Spray Emamectin benzoate at flowering
- **Bird damage:** Guard fields at grain maturity; reflective tape or netting
