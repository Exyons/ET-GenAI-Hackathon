# Wheat - Comprehensive ICAR Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Indian Institute of Wheat & Barley Research (IIWBR), Karnal
**Crop:** Wheat (Triticum aestivum)
**Regions:** Indo-Gangetic Plains — Punjab, Haryana, UP, MP, Rajasthan, Bihar, Gujarat

## Recommended Varieties

### Timely Sown (Nov 1-25)
- **Punjab/Haryana:** HD 3226, PBW 825, WH 1270, DBW 187
- **Uttar Pradesh:** HD 3226, DBW 187, K 1006, HUW 234
- **Madhya Pradesh:** HI 1634, GW 322, JW 3288, MP 4010
- **Rajasthan:** Raj 4238, Raj 4120, HD 2967, WH 1105
- **Bihar:** HD 3226, DBW 187, HUW 234, K 1006

### Late Sown (Dec 1-25)
- HD 3059, PBW 771, DBW 173, WH 1124, NW 1014, UP 2338

### Very Late Sown (after Dec 25)
- HD 3298, WH 1124 — short duration, heat tolerant

## Seed Rate and Treatment
- **Timely sown:** 100 kg/hectare
- **Late sown:** 125 kg/hectare
- **Seed treatment:** Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% (Vitavax Power) at 2.5g/kg seed
- **Bio-agent alternative:** Trichoderma viride at 4g/kg seed

## Sowing
- **Method:** Use seed-cum-fertilizer drill; row spacing 20-22.5 cm
- **Depth:** 5-6 cm (ensure soil contact for germination)
- **Zero tillage:** Use Happy Seeder directly into rice stubble (Punjab/Haryana) — saves time, reduces pollution from stubble burning

## Fertilizer Recommendation (per hectare)
| Condition | N (kg) | P2O5 (kg) | K2O (kg) |
|-----------|--------|-----------|----------|
| Timely sown irrigated | 150 | 60 | 40 |
| Late sown irrigated | 120 | 60 | 40 |
| Rainfed | 60 | 30 | 20 |

- Apply 1/2 N + full P + full K as basal
- Remaining N in 2 equal splits: at first irrigation (CRI) and at tillering
- Apply ZnSO4 at 25 kg/hectare if zinc deficiency is observed (light green leaves)
- Sulphur application: 20 kg/hectare as gypsum for better grain quality

## Irrigation Schedule (Critical Stages)
1. **CRI (Crown Root Initiation):** 20-25 DAS — MOST CRITICAL, skipping causes 30-40% yield loss
2. **Tillering:** 40-45 DAS
3. **Late Jointing:** 60-65 DAS
4. **Flowering:** 80-85 DAS
5. **Milk Stage:** 95-100 DAS
6. **Dough Stage:** 110-115 DAS

**If only 1 irrigation available:** Apply at CRI stage
**If only 3 irrigations:** CRI + Jointing + Milk stage

## Major Diseases

### Yellow/Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis)
- **Symptoms:** Yellow-orange powdery pustules arranged in stripes along leaf veins
- **Favorable conditions:** Cool temperature (10-15°C), high humidity, cloudy weather
- **Treatment:** Spray Propiconazole 25% EC at 1ml/litre OR Tebuconazole 25% EC at 1ml/litre. Two sprays at 15-day interval
- **Critical:** First spray immediately on appearance — delay of even 1 week can cause massive yield loss
- **Resistant varieties:** HD 3226, PBW 825, DBW 187

### Brown/Leaf Rust (Puccinia triticina)
- **Symptoms:** Small, round, orange-brown pustules scattered randomly on leaves
- **Treatment:** Same as yellow rust; spray Propiconazole at 1ml/litre
- **Less critical than yellow rust** but can reduce yield by 10-20%

### Karnal Bunt (Tilletia indica)
- **Symptoms:** Partially bunted grains with black powder and fishy smell; affects wheat at flowering
- **Favorable conditions:** Rainfall/high humidity during flowering (Feb-Mar)
- **Treatment:** No effective in-season treatment. Seed treatment with Thiram is preventive
- **Management:** Use certified disease-free seed; avoid grain from infected fields for seed

### Loose Smut (Ustilago tritici)
- **Symptoms:** Entire ear replaced by black powdery mass; symptoms visible at heading
- **Treatment:** Seed treatment with Carboxin 75% WP at 2.5g/kg seed (systemic action)
- **Prevention:** Only use certified seed; hot water treatment of seed (52°C for 10 min)

### Powdery Mildew (Blumeria graminis)
- **Symptoms:** White powdery patches on leaves, stems, and ears
- **Treatment:** Spray Sulphur 80% WP at 3g/litre OR Propiconazole at 1ml/litre
- **Favorable conditions:** Cool, humid weather with dense canopy

## Major Pests

### Aphids (Sitobion avenae, Rhopalosiphum padi)
- **Symptoms:** Colonies on leaves and ears; honeydew secretion; sooty mold; stunted growth
- **Treatment:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.3ml/litre OR Thiamethoxam 25% WG at 0.3g/litre
- **ETL:** 10-15 aphids per ear head
- **Bio-control:** Encourage lady beetles (Coccinella spp.) — avoid unnecessary insecticide sprays

### Termites (Odontotermes obesus)
- **Symptoms:** Drying of plants in patches; roots hollowed out; soil sheeting on stem base
- **Treatment:** Seed treatment with Chlorpyrifos 20% EC at 4ml/kg seed OR Fipronil 5% SC soil drench
- **Prevention:** Avoid using raw FYM; use well-decomposed compost; irrigate timely

### Pink Borer (Sesamia inferens)
- **Symptoms:** Central shoot drying (dead heart); tunneling in stem
- **Treatment:** Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC at 0.3ml/litre
- **More common in rice-wheat system** where rice stubble harbors larvae

## Weed Management
- **Pre-emergence:** Pendimethalin 30% EC at 3.3 litre/hectare within 2-3 DAS
- **Post-emergence (Grassy weeds):** Clodinafop 15% WP at 400g/hectare at 30-35 DAS
- **Post-emergence (Broad-leaved):** Metsulfuron methyl 20% WP at 20g/hectare at 30-35 DAS
- **Phalaris minor (resistance issue):** Sulfosulfuron 75% WG at 33g/hectare; rotate herbicides annually

## Terminal Heat Stress Management
- **Problem:** Temperatures above 35°C during grain filling (March) cause shriveled grains
- **Solutions:**
  - Sow timely (before Nov 25) to escape terminal heat
  - Use heat-tolerant varieties (HD 3059, DBW 173) for late sowing
  - Light irrigation at grain filling stage
  - Foliar spray of KCl 1% at heading and grain filling
  - Bio-stimulant: Foliar spray of 0.5% ZnSO4 + 0.2% Boric acid at flowering

## Harvest
- Harvest when grain moisture is 12-14%
- Use combine harvester; adjust cylinder speed (600-800 rpm) and concave clearance
- Avoid delays — over-mature wheat shatters and loses grain
- Store at below 12% moisture in jute bags or metal bins
- Fumigate storage with Aluminium phosphide (3 tablets/tonne)
