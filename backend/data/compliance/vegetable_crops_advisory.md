# Vegetable Crops - ICAR Advisory for Major Vegetables

**Source:** ICAR-Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR), Varanasi; ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bangalore
**Crops:** Tomato, Onion, Potato, Chilli, Brinjal, Okra, Cauliflower, Cabbage
**Regions:** All India

---

## Tomato (Lycopersicon esculentum)

### Varieties
- **Open Pollinated:** Pusa Ruby, Pusa Sheetal, Arka Vikas, Arka Saurabh, Kashi Amrit
- **Hybrid:** Arka Rakshak (ToLCV resistant), Kashi Abhiman, US 618, Arka Samrat
- **Cherry Tomato:** Arka Aparna

### Sowing and Transplanting
- Seed rate: 300-400 g/hectare
- Nursery: Sow in raised beds; transplant 25-30 day old seedlings
- Spacing: 60x45 cm (determinate); 75x60 cm (indeterminate)
- Season: Kharif (Jun-Jul), Rabi (Sep-Oct), Summer (Jan-Feb)

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- N:P:K = 120:60:60 kg + FYM 25 tonnes
- Apply Calcium (Gypsum 500 kg/hectare) to prevent Blossom End Rot

### Major Diseases

#### Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV)
- **Symptoms:** Upward curling and crinkling of leaves; stunted plants; no fruit
- **Vector:** Whitefly
- **Management:** Use resistant varieties (Arka Rakshak); control whitefly; yellow sticky traps; rogue infected plants; reflective mulch
- **CRITICAL:** This is the #1 disease of tomato in India — variety selection is key

#### Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- **Symptoms:** Dark brown concentric ring spots (target board pattern) on older leaves; defoliation
- **Treatment:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5g/litre alternated with Chlorothalonil 75% WP at 2g/litre at 10-day intervals

#### Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- **Symptoms:** Water-soaked dark patches on leaves with white cottony growth on underside; rapid field-wide devastation; fruits turn brown
- **Favorable conditions:** Cool, foggy, wet weather (Dec-Jan)
- **Treatment:** Spray Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Ridomil Gold) at 2.5g/litre immediately; Cymoxanil + Mancozeb at 3g/litre
- **Emergency:** If weather is conducive, spray preventively every 7 days

#### Bacterial Wilt (Ralstonia solanacearum)
- **Symptoms:** Sudden permanent wilting without yellowing; bacterial ooze from cut stem in water
- **Treatment:** No effective chemical control
- **Prevention:** Resistant rootstock grafting; Trichoderma + Pseudomonas enriched FYM; soil solarization in summer; avoid waterlogging

### Major Pests
- **Fruit Borer (H. armigera):** Spray Emamectin benzoate 5% SG at 0.4g/litre; install pheromone traps
- **Whitefly:** Spray Spiromesifen at 0.7ml/litre
- **Leaf Miner (Liriomyza trifolii):** Spray Cyromazine 75% WP at 0.5g/litre
- **South American Tomato Moth (Tuta absoluta):** Pheromone traps + Chlorantraniliprole spray at 0.3ml/litre — invasive pest, report sightings to local KVK

## Onion (Allium cepa)

### Varieties
- **Kharif:** Agrifound Dark Red, Bhima Shubhra, Bhima Dark Red, Arka Kalyan
- **Rabi (main season):** Pusa Red, N 53, NHRDF Red, Agrifound Light Red, Bhima Shakti
- **White (for export/dehydration):** Phule Safed, Bhima Safed

### Nursery and Transplanting
- Seed rate: 8-10 kg/hectare
- Nursery: 6-8 weeks; transplant when seedlings are pencil-thick
- Spacing: 15x10 cm (flat beds) or 15x10 cm on ridges
- Rabi planting: December-January; Kharif: June-July

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- N:P:K = 100:50:50 kg
- Sulphur: 40 kg/hectare as elemental sulphur (improves pungency and storage)
- Boron: Foliar spray of Borax 0.2% at 45 and 60 DAT

### Major Diseases

#### Purple Blotch (Alternaria porri)
- **Symptoms:** Purple-brown concentric lesions on leaves and seed stalks; tip die-back
- **Treatment:** Spray Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre + Sticker at first appearance; rotate with Tricyclazole
- **Prevention:** Wide spacing; avoid excess irrigation; crop rotation

#### Stemphylium Leaf Blight
- **Symptoms:** Small light yellow to brown spots that enlarge; leaf tips die back
- **Treatment:** Spray Hexaconazole at 1ml/litre alternated with Mancozeb
- **Often occurs with Purple Blotch** — combined sprays effective

#### Basal Rot (Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. cepae)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing from leaf tips; roots rot; bulb becomes soft and watery from base
- **Treatment:** Seedling root dip in Carbendazim 0.1% before transplanting
- **Prevention:** Crop rotation (3-year break); use raised beds; avoid waterlogging

### Major Pests
- **Thrips (Thrips tabaci):** Most damaging; silvery streaks on leaves; curling. Spray Fipronil 5% SC at 2ml/litre or Spinetoram 11.7% SC at 0.5ml/litre. ETL: 10 thrips/plant
- **Onion Fly:** Root maggots; yellowing; pull test positive. Drench Chlorpyrifos 20% EC at 2ml/litre

### Storage
- Cure onions in shade for 7-10 days until neck dries
- Store in well-ventilated structures; bottom-ventilated racks
- Spray Maleic Hydrazide (MH) at 3000 ppm 15 days before harvest to prevent sprouting in storage

## Potato (Solanum tuberosum)

### Varieties
- **Table purpose:** Kufri Jyoti, Kufri Pukhraj, Kufri Badshah, Kufri Sindhuri
- **Processing (chips):** Kufri Chipsona-1, Kufri Chipsona-3, Kufri Frysona
- **Early maturing (75-80 days):** Kufri Pukhraj, Kufri Ashoka, Kufri Khyati
- **Late blight resistant:** Kufri Garima, Kufri Arun

### Planting
- Seed rate: 25-30 quintals/hectare (medium-sized tubers 30-40g)
- Spacing: 60x20 cm (medium tubers); 60x15 cm (small tubers)
- Depth: 5-7 cm in furrows
- Seed treatment: Mancozeb 0.25% dip for 10 minutes; air dry before planting
- Season: Oct-Nov (plains); Feb-Mar (hills)

### Fertilizer (per hectare)
- N:P:K = 180:80:100 kg
- Apply 2/3 N + full P + 1/2 K at planting
- Remaining N at earthing up (30 DAP)
- Remaining K at second earthing up (45 DAP)
- Zinc: 25 kg/hectare ZnSO4 if deficient

### Major Diseases

#### Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- **MOST DESTRUCTIVE DISEASE — can destroy entire crop in 7-10 days**
- **Symptoms:** Water-soaked dark patches on leaves with white sporulation; rapid defoliation; tubers show brown rot
- **Favorable conditions:** Temperature 10-20°C, RH >90%, foggy nights, drizzle
- **Treatment:** Spray Cymoxanil + Mancozeb at 3g/litre OR Metalaxyl + Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre every 7 days during conducive weather
- **Preventive:** Start Mancozeb sprays from 45 DAP at 10-day intervals
- **WARNING:** Late blight can also spread through infected seed tubers — use certified seed only

#### Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- **Symptoms:** Concentric ring spots on older leaves; premature defoliation; reduces tuber size
- **Treatment:** Spray Mancozeb at 2.5g/litre from 45 DAP

#### Black Scurf (Rhizoctonia solani)
- **Symptoms:** Black/dark brown sclerotia on tuber surface; canker on stolons; misshapen tubers
- **Treatment:** Seed treatment with Boric acid 3% dip for 30 minutes OR Pencycuron 22.9% SC at 1.3ml/litre
- **Prevention:** Crop rotation; avoid fields with high inoculum

### Major Pests
- **Aphids (Myzus persicae):** Vector for viruses (PVY, PLRV). Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.3ml/litre. For seed crop: haulm (top) destruction when aphid population crosses threshold
- **White Grub:** Soil application of Chlorpyrifos 10G at 25 kg/hectare
- **Potato Tuber Moth (Phthorimaea operculella):** Mines in leaves and tubers. Store tubers covered with dried Lantana/Eucalyptus leaves. Spray Quinalphos 25% EC at 2ml/litre in field

### Harvest and Storage
- Haulm cutting 10-15 days before harvest (skin curing, virus prevention in seed crop)
- Harvest when soil is dry; avoid tuber injury
- Grade tubers; remove cut/damaged ones
- Cold storage at 2-4°C, 90-95% RH for table purpose
- Processed varieties: store at 8-12°C (prevents sugar accumulation)
- Apply CIPC (Chlorpropham) in cold stores to prevent sprouting

## Chilli (Capsicum annuum)

### Varieties
- **Green chilli:** Kashi Anmol, Pusa Jwala, Arka Suphal
- **Dry chilli:** Byadgi, Guntur Sannam (S4), Teja, LCA 334
- **High capsaicin:** Bhut Jolokia, Naga Morich (Northeast India)

### Major Diseases
- **Anthracnose/Die-back (Colletotrichum capsici):** Sunken spots on fruits; tip die-back. Spray Carbendazim 1g/litre + Mancozeb 2.5g/litre
- **Leaf Curl (Begomovirus):** Transmitted by whitefly; leaf curling, stunting. Resistant varieties + whitefly control
- **Bacterial Wilt:** Same management as tomato — Trichoderma, soil solarization, resistant rootstock
- **Powdery Mildew:** White powder on leaves. Spray Sulphur 80% WP at 3g/litre

### Major Pests
- **Thrips:** Most damaging; leaf curling, bronzing. Spray Fipronil 5% SC at 2ml/litre
- **Fruit Borer:** Spray Emamectin benzoate at 0.4g/litre
- **Mites:** Downward leaf curling; spray Propargite 57% EC at 2ml/litre or Spiromesifen

## Okra / Bhindi (Abelmoschus esculentus)

### Varieties
- **YVMV Resistant:** Arka Anamika, Kashi Pragati, Kashi Kranti, Pusa Sawani
- **Hybrid:** HOK-152, Mahyco No. 10

### Key Disease
- **Yellow Vein Mosaic Virus (YVMV):** Yellow network of veins on leaves; stunted plants. Vector: Whitefly. **Only prevention is resistant varieties + whitefly control.** Rogue out infected plants

### Key Pest
- **Shoot and Fruit Borer (Earias vittella):** Drooping of young shoots; bore holes in fruits. Spray Emamectin benzoate at 0.4g/litre. Remove and destroy infested shoots/fruits

## General IPM Practices for Vegetables
1. **Nursery:** Use nylon net (40-mesh) cover to prevent whitefly/thrips entry
2. **Trap crops:** Plant marigold borders (traps Helicoverpa); basil repels whitefly
3. **Yellow sticky traps:** 25-30 per hectare for whitefly/aphid monitoring
4. **Pheromone traps:** 5 per hectare for Helicoverpa, Spodoptera monitoring
5. **Bio-pesticides:** Neem oil 1500 ppm at 3ml/litre as first line; Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis) at 1g/litre for caterpillars
6. **Soil solarization:** Cover moist soil with transparent polyethylene (25 micron) for 30-45 days in summer — controls soil-borne diseases, nematodes, and weeds
7. **Mulching:** Silver-black plastic mulch reflects UV light (repels whitefly) + conserves moisture + suppresses weeds
