# Organic Farming - ICAR & NPOF Advisory

**Source:** ICAR-Indian Institute of Farming Systems Research (IIFSR), Modipuram; National Programme for Organic Production (NPOP); Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)
**Applicable To:** All crops, All regions

## Government Schemes for Organic Farming
- **PKVY:** Rs 50,000/hectare over 3 years for cluster-based organic farming
- **MOVCDNER:** Rs 25,000/hectare for organic farming in Northeast India
- **Contact:** District Agriculture Officer or nearest KVK for enrollment

## Conversion Period
- Minimum 3 years conversion from conventional to certified organic
- During conversion: follow organic practices but cannot label/sell as "organic"
- Maintain buffer zone of 25 feet from conventional fields
- Keep detailed records of all inputs used

## Organic Nutrient Management

### Farmyard Manure (FYM)
- Apply well-decomposed FYM at 10-15 tonnes/hectare
- **Decomposition:** Pile for 3-4 months minimum; turn every 15 days
- Never use fresh/raw FYM — it attracts termites, causes nitrogen lock-up, and may contain weed seeds
- Nutrient content: 0.5% N, 0.2% P2O5, 0.5% K2O (approximate)

### Vermicompost
- Best organic manure — concentrated nutrients + beneficial microbes + growth hormones
- Apply 5 tonnes/hectare
- Produce on-farm: Use Eisenia fetida (red wiggler) earthworms
- Bedding: Crop residues + cow dung + vegetable waste
- Ready in 45-60 days; sieve and use
- Nutrient content: 1.5% N, 0.5% P2O5, 0.8% K2O

### Jeevamrutha (Traditional preparation)
- **Recipe:** 10 kg fresh cow dung + 10 litres cow urine + 2 kg jaggery + 2 kg pulse flour (besan) + handful of soil from under a tree + 200 litres water
- Ferment for 5-7 days; stir daily
- Apply at 500 litres/hectare through irrigation or drench
- Activates soil microbial life; provides nutrients
- Apply every 15 days during crop growth

### Panchagavya
- **Recipe:** Fresh cow dung (7 kg) + cow urine (3 litres) + cow milk (2 litres) + curd (2 litres) + ghee (1 kg) + sugarcane juice (3 litres) + tender coconut water (3 litres) + ripe banana (12 nos) + water (to make 20 litres)
- Ferment for 15-21 days; stir twice daily
- Use as foliar spray at 3% (30 ml/litre)
- Growth promoter + pest repellent + beneficial microbes

### Green Manuring
- **Dhaincha (Sesbania aculeata):** Best green manure crop; fixes 80-100 kg N/hectare; incorporate at 45-50 DAS
- **Sunhemp (Crotalaria juncea):** Fixes 60-80 kg N; also suppresses nematodes
- **Gliricidia:** Perennial; use as hedge row; cut and incorporate
- **In-situ green manuring:** Grow between main crop rows and incorporate

## Organic Pest Management

### Neem-based Preparations
- **Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) 5%:** Crush 50g neem kernels, soak overnight in 1 litre water, filter, add soap solution (1g/litre). Effective against caterpillars, aphids, whitefly, leafhoppers
- **Neem Oil 1%:** 10 ml commercial neem oil (1500 ppm azadirachtin) per litre water + soap. Broad-spectrum insecticide and fungicide
- **Neem Cake:** Apply 250 kg/hectare in soil — controls soil pests (nematodes, white grubs, termites) + slow-release nitrogen

### Biological Control Agents
- **Trichoderma viride/harzianum:** Seed treatment (10g/kg seed) + soil application (2.5 kg/hectare mixed with FYM). Controls soil-borne diseases (wilt, root rot, damping off)
- **Pseudomonas fluorescens:** Seed treatment (10g/kg) + soil drench. Controls bacterial wilt, blast
- **Beauveria bassiana:** Spray 5g/litre. Controls borers, grubs, whitefly
- **Metarhizium anisopliae:** Spray 5g/litre. Controls termites, grubs, leafhoppers
- **Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt):** Spray 1-2g/litre. Controls all caterpillar pests (bollworm, fruit borer, leaf folder)
- **NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus):** Available for Helicoverpa and Spodoptera. Spray 250 LE/hectare in evening
- **Trichogramma (egg parasitoid):** Release 50,000-1,50,000/hectare. Controls stem borers, fruit borers

### Trap-based Methods
- **Pheromone traps:** Species-specific lures available for major pests. Install 5/hectare
- **Yellow sticky traps:** For whitefly, aphids, leaf miners. Install 25-30/hectare at canopy height
- **Blue sticky traps:** For thrips. Install 25/hectare
- **Light traps:** For moths (stem borer, bollworm). Install 1/hectare at 2m height; operate 6-10 PM

### Traditional Preparations
- **Dashparni Ark:** Extract of 10 leaves (neem, karanj, castor, etc.) + cow urine + chilli + garlic. Broad pest repellent
- **Handi Trap:** Earthen pot with jaggery + yeast solution — attracts and traps fruit flies (for mango, guava, cucurbits)
- **Buttermilk spray (5%):** Controls powdery mildew on cucurbits and grapes
- **Cow urine spray (10%):** General pest repellent + foliar nutrition

## Organic Weed Management
- **Mulching:** Crop residue mulch (5-10 cm thick) OR biodegradable mulch film
- **Solarization:** Transparent polythene cover on moist soil for 45 days (summer) — kills weed seeds in top 5 cm
- **Stale seed bed:** Irrigate, let weeds germinate, destroy mechanically, then sow crop
- **Cover crops:** Cowpea, horsegram as living mulch between rows
- **Mechanical:** Hand weeding, wheel hoe, power weeder — primary weed management in organic farming
- **Crop rotation:** Break weed cycles by alternating row crops and broadcast crops

## Organic Certification
1. **Third Party Certification:** Through APEDA-accredited agencies (costly, for export)
2. **Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India):** Group certification, farmer-led, free/low-cost, valid domestically
   - Form a group of 5+ farmers
   - Register at pgsindia-ncof.gov.in
   - Maintain records for 3 years
   - Peer inspection by group members
   - Get PGS-India Green (in conversion) or PGS-India Organic certificate
3. **Jaivik Kheti Portal (jaivikkheti.in):** Online marketplace for certified organic produce

## Crop Rotation for Organic Farms
- **Principle:** Cereal → Legume → Oilseed → Vegetable (4-year rotation)
- **Example (irrigated):** Rice (kharif) → Wheat (rabi) → Green manure (summer) → Maize (kharif) → Chickpea (rabi)
- **Example (rainfed):** Soybean (kharif) → Wheat (rabi) → Fallow/green manure → Cotton (kharif) → Chickpea (rabi)
- Include at least one legume crop every year for biological nitrogen fixation
